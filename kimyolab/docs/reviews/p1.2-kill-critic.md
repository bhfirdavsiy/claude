### Topilma: `READY` so‘zi ikki xil ma’noni yashira olardi

`LearningActivityReadiness.status==='READY'` kodda faqat **texnik launchability**ni bildirardi: lifecycle `ready` va bitta valid route.
Inson approval’i esa alohida mavjud edi: `releaseReady` boolean va `ACTIVITY_REVIEW_PENDING` reason. Launch mantig‘ida ikki tushuncha aralashmagan edi.
Lekin hisobot va pack’da yolg‘iz `status: READY` / `ready: 118` yozilardi. 146 ta activity’ning hammasi inson review’ini kutayotgan bo‘lsa ham, “118 READY” pedagogik approval deb o‘qilishi mumkin edi.
Bu semantik blocker deb baholandi va merge’dan oldin tuzatildi.

**FIXED:**
- **Ikki o‘lcham.** Readiness ikki mustaqil o‘lchamga ajratildi (`runtime` + `content`). `status` va `releaseReady` maydonlari olib tashlandi, pack schema `kimyolab.activity-readiness.v2`.
  - `runtime`: `READY`/`PENDING`/`DISABLED`/`BLOCKED`.
  - `content`: `APPROVED`/`REVIEW_PENDING`/`REJECTED`. Bu yangi taksonomiya emas: mavjud per-rol `ApprovalStatus` (`pending`/`approved`/`rejected`, hash-pinned) agregatsiyasi.
- **Launch qoidasi.** Launch `runtime` bo‘yicha qaror qilinadi. Yagona istisno: inson **REJECTED** qilgan kontent hech qachon ochilmaydi.
- **Hisobot.** Impact hisobotida `semantics` izohi va `content` bloki bor: `approved 0`, `reviewPending 146`, `runtimeReadyButNotApproved 118`, `releaseReady 0`.
