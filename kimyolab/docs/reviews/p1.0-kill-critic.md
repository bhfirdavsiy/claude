## `assessment_complete` semantikasi (§2–§3)

**Topilma.** P1.0 hisobotidagi parity oqimi ikkala yo‘lda ham haqiqiy objective assessment submit qilardi. Bu yo‘llar `LearningRunner.run` → `submitAssessment` va `BrowserProgressService.submitAssessment` edi, `ev.a1` esa `concept-assessment` item edi. Shuning uchun parity’dagi `assessment_complete` reflection’dan emas, baholangan assessment’dan kelgan. Hisobotdagi “OPEN → THEORY → PRACTICE → evidence → reinforcement” ro‘yxati assessment qadamini tushirib qoldirgani uchun noto‘g‘ri edi.

Lekin audit haqiqiy semantik teshiklarni topdi (F1, F2, F3). Ular endi yopilgan:

| Event | Ma’nosi | Achievement |
|---|---|---|
| `THEORY_COMPLETED` | nazariya tugadi | `in_progress` |
| `PRACTICE_COMPLETED` | practice natijasi `complete` | `practice_complete` |
| `REINFORCEMENT_COMPLETED` | reflection/mustahkamlash yozildi | `in_progress` (hech qachon assessment emas) |
| `ASSESSMENT_SUBMITTED` | objective assessment topshirildi | yo‘q (`cycle.assessment.complete=false`) |
| `ASSESSMENT_EVALUATED` | baholandi, `objectiveItems ≥ 1` | `assessment_complete` — **yagona manba** |
| `MASTERY_UPDATED` | baholangan unit’ni aniqlashtiradi | `mastered` / `needs_review`, faqat rank ≥ 3 bo‘lsa |
| `ACHIEVEMENT_CONTEXT_CHANGED` | content version almashdi | compatible bo‘lmasa arxiv + `in_progress` |

`progress.status`, `activeStage`, `DisplayStatus` va `Attempt.status` to‘rt xil narsani ifodalaydi:

- `progress.status` — unit achievement.
- `activeStage` — o‘quv siklidagi joriy bosqich (`theory` → `practice` → `reinforcement` → `complete`).
- `DisplayStatus` — o‘quvchiga ko‘rsatiladigan label.
- `Attempt.status` — bitta urinishning holati.

`assessment_complete` endi faqat “objective assessment baholandi” degan ma’noni bildiradi. U “sikl tugadi” degani emas: siklning tugashi `isLearningUnitComplete` / `activeStage==='complete'` bilan aniqlanadi. Shuning uchun nomini o‘zgartirish kerak emas edi, faqat uni yaratadigan yo‘llar tuzatildi.

Legacy (P0) yozuvlarda reflection `assessment_complete` sifatida saqlangan. Ular `cycle.assessment` bo‘lmagani sababli aniqlanadi va `reinforcement_complete` deb ko‘rsatiladi. Schema migratsiyasi talab qilinmadi.

Testlar:
- `p1-event-taxonomy`: “only an evaluated objective assessment…”, “renamed/unknown events fail closed”.
- `p1-closeout-invariants`: §2/§3.
- `p1-orchestrator-contract`: “parity flow WITHOUT an objective assessment never reaches assessment_complete”.

## Maxsus ssenariylar

- **§4 Zero-evidence attempt — Variant B tanlandi.** Attempt faqat birinchi evidence’da yoki yakunlanganda saqlanadi. Sahifani ochib, hech narsa qilmay chiqish attempt qoldirmaydi, ya’ni phantom attempt yo‘q. Bitta bo‘lsa ham evidence bo‘lsa, attempt `abandoned` bo‘lib qoladi va dalillar tarixda saqlanadi, ya’ni meaningful attempt yo‘qolmaydi. Test: “zero-evidence page visit leaves NO attempt…”.
- **§5 Navigation/unload.**
  - Unload paytida async IndexedDB yozuviga **tayanilmaydi**.
  - SPA ichida route change, Back va Forward holatida `LEAVE_PRACTICE` ishlaydi.
  - Refresh, tab yoki window yopilishi va crash holatida: sahifa har bir ochiq attempt uchun Web Lock ushlab turadi. Sahifa o‘lganda platforma lock’ni bo‘shatadi. Keyingi boot’da `recoverOrphanedAttempts` hech bir tirik sahifaga tegishli bo‘lmagan `in_progress` attempt’larni `abandoned` qiladi. Boshqa ochiq tab’ning attempt’iga tegilmaydi.
  - Web Locks bo‘lmagan platformada faqat 24 soatdan eski attempt’lar tiklanadi.
  - E2E testlar: internal route, Back, Forward, refresh, tab close, window (popup) close, parallel tab.
  - **RISK (hujjatlashtirilgan):** recovered attempt’ning `completedAt` vaqti — tiklash vaqti, sahifadan chiqilgan aniq vaqt emas. Keyingi boot bo‘lmaguncha attempt `in_progress` ko‘rinadi.
- **§6 Terminal invariant.** `complete → abandon` va `abandon → complete` ikkala tartibda ham orkestrator va store darajasida rad etiladi.
- **§7 Dedupe.** Dedupe kaliti bitta attempt ichida engine’dagi source identity (`sourceEvidenceId`) va `createdAt` dan tashqari butun mazmun. Step A va step B bir xil score va javob bilan alohida qoladi. Bir xil source’dan kelgan yangi javob yangi evidence hisoblanadi.
- **§8 Mastery cache.** Cache o‘chirildi → qayta hisoblandi → natija canonical qiymatga teng. Undefined old content version evidence tarixda qoladi, `excludedEvidenceIds` da ko‘rinadi va hisobga kirmaydi.
- **§9 Monotonlik scope’i.** Bitta context ichida retry yoki muvaffaqiyatsiz urinish achievement’ni pasaytirmaydi. Yangi content version `compatible` deb e’lon qilinmagan bo‘lsa, eski achievement va cycle state `cycle.archive.<version>@<vaqt>` ga ko‘chiriladi (audit uchun), unit esa yangi context’da qayta boshlanadi. `recalculable` ham achievement’ni olib o‘tmaydi: evidence qayta skorlanadi, lekin achievement’ni faqat yangi baholash beradi.

## 8 ta savol

### 1. Orchestratorni chetlab state yozish mumkinmi? — **FIXED**
- **Evidence:** progress, attempt, evidence va mastery yozuvi faqat `LearningOrchestrator` → `LearningStorePort` orqali o‘tadi. AST guard mutation metodlariga har qanday murojaatni ushlaydi: chaqiruv, property o‘qish, `bind`, `['literal']` va destructuring. Boundary funksiyalarni alias yoki namespace import orqali chaqirish va re-export orqali “yuvish” ham ushlanadi. Bu tekshiruv `lint` ichida ishlaydi.
- **Test:** `p1-architecture-guard` — 6 ta test, shu jumladan “guard bypass attempts are caught…” va P0 fayllari ustida “not vacuous” testi.
- **Remaining limitation:** bu sandbox emas, lint. Hisoblangan dinamik kalit (`store[name]()`) va `any` orqali uzatilgan store statik aniqlanmaydi. `features/labs` dagi external evidence o‘z store metodidan (`saveExternalEvidence`) foydalanadi. U canonical practice/assessment tarixidan tashqarida va ADR’da qayd etilgan.

### 2. Bitta click yangi Attempt yaratishi mumkinmi? — **PASS**
- **Evidence:** attempt identity `BEGIN_PRACTICE` da bir marta belgilanadi. `APPLY_PRACTICE_COMMAND` attempt yaratmaydi. Brauzerda bitta sahifa = bitta sessiya (`bootstrap.ts`).
- **Test:** “10 UI commands … = exactly 1 Attempt” (haqiqiy calc 7.5 kontenti), E2E “several answers … = one attempt”, E2E Forward = yangi sahifa sessiyasi = yangi attempt.
- **Remaining limitation:** yo‘q.

### 3. Parallel event progress yo‘qotishi mumkinmi? — **PASS**
- **Evidence:** `updateProgress` bitta `readwrite` tranzaksiyada ishlaydi.
- **Test:** 100 ta parallel update’dan 100 tasi saqlanadi (eski pattern bilan 1 tasi saqlanardi). Ikki orkestrator poygasi testi ham bor.
- **Remaining limitation:** bir nechta tab bir xil IndexedDB’da ishlashi ham tranzaksiya bilan serializatsiya qilinadi. Cross-device sync yo‘q (scope’dan tashqarida).

### 4. Evidence ikki marta persist bo‘lishi mumkinmi? — **PASS**
- **Evidence:** attempt ichida semantik dedupe ishlaydi. Store faqat `add` qiladi (overwrite yo‘q, `EVIDENCE_ID_COLLISION`). Abandoned attempt’ga evidence qo‘shilmaydi.
- **Test:** dedupe testlari (§7), duplication kontrakt testi, abandoned attempt’ga append rad etilishi.
- **Remaining limitation:** ikki *turli* attempt’dagi bir xil javob ikki alohida dalil hisoblanadi. Bu ataylab qilingan: har bir urinish mustaqil kuzatuv.

### 5. Eski version evidence yangi mastery’ga kirishi mumkinmi? — **PASS**
- **Evidence:** mastery faqat aniq `MasteryContext` bilan hisoblanadi (guard: `MASTERY_WITHOUT_CONTEXT`). Undefined version’lar `incompatible` hisoblanadi (fail-safe). Concept migration ham context talab qiladi (C7).
- **Test:** §8 cache + incompatible evidence testi, “version context” kontrakt testi, `phase11-concept-migration` bo‘yicha C7 regression testlari.
- **Remaining limitation:** `recalculable` evidence qayta skorlanadi. Bu kontent muallifining ongli e’loni (`evidenceCompatibility`).

### 6. Mastered unit retry sabab regress qiladimi? — **FIXED** (scope aniqlashtirildi)
- **Evidence:** bitta compatibility context ichida regress qilmaydi. Yangi context’da eski achievement arxivlanadi (F5).
- **Test:** “monotonic mastered → retry” (kontrakt), “a new, undeclared content version does NOT carry…”, “a declared compatible content version keeps…”.
- **Remaining limitation:** `needs_review` `assessment_complete` o‘rnini egallashi mumkin (bir xil rank). Bu mastery signali, regress emas.

### 7. Runner va browser bir xil scenario’da turli state hosil qiladimi? — **PASS**
- **Evidence:** ikkala facade ham bitta orkestratorga delegatsiya qiladi.
- **Test:** “adapter parity” normalizatsiyadan keyin progress, attempts, evidence, mastery va assessments bo‘yicha deep-equal. Assessment’siz parity testi ham bor.
- **Remaining limitation:** normalizatsiya UUID va vaqt tamg‘alarini olib tashlaydi (ular ataylab har xil).

### 8. Test implementationni emas, invariantni tekshiryaptimi? — **PASS**
- **Evidence:** testlar kuzatiladigan natijani tekshiradi: store snapshot, attempt holati, E2E’dagi haqiqiy IndexedDB. Ichki metodlar yoki private state tekshirilmaydi. Race testining “tishi borligi” eski pattern bilan isbotlangan: natija 1 chiqadi. Guard testi haqiqiy P0 fayllarida violation topadi.
- **Remaining limitation:** P0 dan qolgan ikkita test (`phase6b-progress`, `p0-attempt-sessions`) bootstrap wiring’ni regex bilan tekshiradi. Ular E2E bilan qoplangan, lekin o‘zlari implementation’ga bog‘langan.
