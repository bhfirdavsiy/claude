- 21 juftlikdan faqat 3 tasi modellashtirilgan (AgNO₃+NaCl, BaCl₂+H₂SO₄, ZnCl₂+NaOH). Qolganlari fail-closed “not modeled”; masalan AgNO₃+BaCl₂ haqiqatda cho‘kma beradi, lekin KB’da yo‘q.
- KB’da aniq no-reaction yozuvi yo‘q.
- Uchala cho‘kma ham oq. Ular turli reaksiya va turli net-ion tenglamalar (turli domain holati), lekin ko‘rinadigan rang bir xil.
- Tokcha content (7 reagent, pending review). HCl qasddan kiritilmagan: `rxn.naoh-hcl` kuzatuvi KB’da “color-change: changed” (indikatorsiz shubhali), chemistry reviewer ko‘rishi kerak.
